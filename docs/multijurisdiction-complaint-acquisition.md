# Multijurisdiction Complaint Acquisition

Atlas Domain 3 treats complaint and service-request records as source observations, not as findings of wrongdoing.

Initial governed sources:

- CFPB Consumer Complaint Database — nationwide
- Washington Attorney General Consumer Protection complaints
- Oregon consumer complaint public data
- New York City 311 service requests
- Chicago 311 service requests

Each adapter must preserve stable source identity, source provenance, jurisdiction, observed time, actor/business/agency fields when present, and the source's allegation/complaint status. A source observation does not become a civic signal until a declared deterministic population detector derives one.

The initial cross-jurisdiction acquisition contract exists to support repeat-entity, geographic-cluster, temporal-change, cross-category, and later relationship/ownership analysis without collapsing independent source records together.
